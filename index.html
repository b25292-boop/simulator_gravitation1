✅ Result

The gravitational force between two objects increases when either M₁ or M₂ is increased. The force decreases when the distance between the objects increases. Therefore, F ∝ M₁M₂ and F ∝ 1/d². Hence, Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation is verified.
